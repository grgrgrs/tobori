## What this is
Neuroscience seeks to understand how biological brains generate perception, memory, decision-making, and self-awareness. It explores how neural activity encodes meaning and adapts through learning. When used as a model for mind, neuroscience provides a blueprint for how intelligence might arise from interconnected systems without a central controller.
## Why it matters
Artificial intelligence increasingly draws from neuroscience to build more flexible, adaptable, and human-like systems. Concepts like attention, memory, and even emotional modulation are being mimicked in neural networks and embodied agents. By studying how the brain works, AI researchers gain insights into architectures and mechanisms that could make artificial minds more capable—and more aligned with how humans think.
