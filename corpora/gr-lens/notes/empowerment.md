## What this is
Despite all the talk about AI futures, there is little that helps the average person understand what AI can do for them now, and how to anticipate what comes next. From assistance with writing to intelligent web search to assistive devices, there are many real-world needs where AI can help people today. But it is challenging for the average person to get a read on what's real today, let alone keep up with the rapid pace of change.
## Why it matters to me
I want to help people with practical and demystified use of AI. I see how it helps me, and want to help others to have the advantages I have received from using of AI.
