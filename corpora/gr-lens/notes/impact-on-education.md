## What this is
AI will have significant effects on human education. It offers new tools and methods of learning. AI tutors could fill teaching gaps. As a case study, it upends computer science education due to the significant abilities of AI to write code.
## Why it matters to me
I want to help humans adapt to AI in ways that enrich lives, not just threaten jobs. I believe it should be possible to posit positive outcomes in every field. Education is a good starting point as it marches on in yearly cycles, with chances to reinvent itself each year.
