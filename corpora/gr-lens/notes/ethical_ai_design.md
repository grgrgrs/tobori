## What this is
How AI can be designed to best serve, and not be a threat to humanity. AI may also evolve to have ideas of its own about how to decide what is best.
Includes topics such as Asimov's three robotic laws, etc.
## Why it matters
There is a lot of concern and fear about what AI might evolve to do, and whether it could become a threat to humanity. This concern will affect how mankind sees and works with AI.
