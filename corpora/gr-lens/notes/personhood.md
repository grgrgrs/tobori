## What this is

This theme questions the boundary between artificial and human identity. It investigates whether and how AI might qualify as an autonomous agent or moral being. Topics include selfhood, free will, and whether an AI can be a peer rather than a puppet. It includes articles that anthropomorphize AI agents, and examines our psychological or emotional relationships with increasingly autonomous systems.

## Why it matters to me

These questions challenge how I think about mind, autonomy, and moral responsibility. Whether or not AI truly becomes sentient, how we relate to it—and project identity onto it—shapes both our technology and our humanity. Wrestling with these ambiguities helps me stay alert to ethical blind spots.
