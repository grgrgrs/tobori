## What this is
This topic tracks companies across industries and geographies that are actively incorporating AI into their core business strategies and operations. It focuses less on “AI companies” per se and more on firms — large or small — that are starting to _leverage AI in meaningful, material ways_. These may include new product directions, efficiency gains, shifts in customer experience, or structural repositioning to take advantage of emerging AI capabilities. Coverage may range from earnings calls and executive strategy to case studies or investor commentary, with a preference for substance over speculation.
Examples:
- Nvidia - obvious
- Embraer embracing AI for e.g. pilotless aircraft
- Eve, Embraer spin-off use of AI in VTOL aircraft
## Why it matters to me
I'm watching for signs that a company understands how AI can transform key aspects of its business — and is actually doing something about it. As both an investor and a builder of AI tools, I'm drawn to companies with vision, but not just talk: I want to see concrete steps, strategic alignment, and a capacity to execute. I’m particularly interested in firms that may not be well-known AI players but are making smart, early moves that could give them an edge. I find the space rich with potential and innovation — though I stay skeptical of hype, jargon, and surface-level claims.
