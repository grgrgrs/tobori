## What this is
This theme captures tools and breakthroughs in creative AI—spanning text, image, music, architecture, and especially video. It includes both real-time generation and long-form synthesis, often using diffusion models, transformers, or multimodal pipelines.
## Why it matters
AI is reshaping creative industries and enabling solo creators to do the work of teams. It also raises questions about originality, copyright, and what it means to “create.”
The best outputs push boundaries in storytelling, aesthetics, and user imagination.
