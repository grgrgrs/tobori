## What this is

This theme explores anticipated cultural and behavioral changes emerging from widespread AI integration, often framed through speculative or near-future lenses. It considers how AI could reshape American life, work, and social fabric over the next 5–10 years.

## Why it matters to me

AI's impact isn't limited to technical systems—it shifts how people live, relate, and make meaning. I find myself returning to questions about what changes are underway now and how they ripple outward. These ideas help frame both concerns and hopes for the next decade.
