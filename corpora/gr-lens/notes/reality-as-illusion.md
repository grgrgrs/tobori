## What this is

This theme examines the tension between surface appearances and deeper truths—between how things seem and what they are. The Buddhist perspective—that the self is a constructed illusion—offers a parallel critique of assumptions in AI philosophy.

## Why it matters to me

This theme resonates with a recurring curiosity: when is what I see or believe merely an illusion? What lies beneath our perceptions? These questions matter in both spiritual practice and critical thinking.
Helps reframe questions like “is AI sentient?” into more useful inquiries about patterns of cognition, causality, and awareness.
