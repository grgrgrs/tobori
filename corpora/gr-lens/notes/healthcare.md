## What this is
Doctors and staff spend a lot of time recording notes and subsequent medical coding. AI can help in diagnostic tasks (reading x-rays, interpreting samples.) Medicare Recovery Audit Contractors and similar entities spend a great deal of their staff time on review of medical records to assess proper coding and payment. There is a great opportunity for AI to assist and augment human capabilities.
## Why it matters to me
I have worked in this field before and may have the opportunity to do so again. I understand the data and subject matter, and would like the opportunity to play a role in the development of AI-augmented medical records review.
