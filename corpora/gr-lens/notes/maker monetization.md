## What this is
AI tools allow individuals to create applications that previously took a team. It also supports very fast iterations, critical to evolving and tuning a highly interactive application. One person with vision and basic technical awareness can easily play the role of Product Manager, Product Architect, Engineering Manager, Software Developer, Dev Ops Engineer, and QA Engineer. The opportunities to create new things just expanded massively.
## Why it matters to me
I have a solid working knowledge of all of the spectrum of skills required to envision and develop applications. Essentially I have a team of people working for me in product and engineering that can, at my direction, create new applications very quickly. I want to use my significant skills in not only technology, but subject matter, UX/UI design, and data analytics to brings applications to life and try to make money from selling or sharing them.
