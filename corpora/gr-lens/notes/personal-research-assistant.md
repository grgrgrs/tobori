## What this is
Any current research topic can be overwhelming when trying to keep up with a daily deluge of articles, blogs, and other material on the web. A personal research assistant will understand my interests and review hundreds or thousands of articles a day in order to bring to my attention those of most interest. It includes summarization pipelines, headless browsers, content deduplication, intelligent feeds, and AI that acts as a filter or curator rather than a creator.
These systems aim to restore clarity and relevance in a noisy digital environment.
## Why it matters to me
I have built Tobiri, an AI Research Assistant and continue to refine its capabilities. While largely a learning exercise, I am excited to employ my assistant in real life. It will digest news feeds, and compare articles to a personal corpus of my interests (of which this document is a part.) It will assess each articles relevance to my corpus and present to me the most interesting articles. Tools that help users find signal amid noise can unlock productivity, improve decision-making, and mitigate fatigue. This theme is central to building trusted systems in an era of overwhelming information volume.
