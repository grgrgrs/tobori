## What this is
Focuses on AI agents that serve intimate, ongoing roles in people’s lives—from productivity tools and writing assistants to romantic partners and digital confidants. It includes experiments with emotionally responsive bots, character agents, and anthropomorphized interfaces.
## Why it matters
These systems shape how humans relate to machines—emotionally, socially, and cognitively. Companion AIs reveal both the promise of personalized support and the psychological risks of attachment, dependency, and blurred identity boundaries. Many people are lonely; AI might help.
