## What this is
This theme covers the emergence of AI-integrated consumer devices, including smart glasses, wearable assistants, and XR/AR hardware. These products aim to bring context-aware intelligence into everyday life—whether for productivity, navigation, recording, or fitness optimization.  Exploring how real-time information access through smart glasses could today change the lives for the better of many vision impaired people, and in the future offer assistance with other disabilities.
## Why it matters
I've worked with people with limited eyesight and have seen how Meta AI Glasses have made a significant difference in their lives.
These devices mark a shift from screen-based to ambient and embodied AI interaction.
Their success will influence public familiarity with AI, real-world data collection, and human-device symbiosis.
They also signal where the AI-human interface is heading—hands-free, personalized, and constantly present.
