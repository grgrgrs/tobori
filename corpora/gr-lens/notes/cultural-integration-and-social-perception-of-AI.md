## What this is
This theme captures how AI is perceived, interpreted, and absorbed into popular culture, humor, and public discourse. It includes memes, social commentary,  and creative reinterpretations of AI's role in society—often blending critique, fascination, and satire. It reflects the evolving public imagination around what AI is and could be.
## Why it matters
Public sentiment plays a critical role in shaping regulatory responses, consumer adoption, and funding trends. Cultural artifacts—whether satirical or sincere—help gauge how AI is being understood outside technical or business circles. Tracking this theme can surface early signs of backlash, hype fatigue, or cultural acceptance that may influence product viability or policy.
