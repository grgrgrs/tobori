## What this is
This theme focuses on how humans and AI collaborate at scale to make better decisions, generate insights, or guide models. It includes human-in-the-loop feedback, collective forecasting, crowd labeling, and swarm intelligence systems that harness distributed knowledge.

## Why it matters
AI systems that integrate human input often outperform fully autonomous models, especially in domains requiring judgment, context, or adaptation. Understanding how to balance autonomy and oversight is key to building safe, high-impact systems in science, governance, and creative work.
