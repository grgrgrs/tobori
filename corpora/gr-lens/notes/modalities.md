## What this is
This theme explores the rise of AI systems that can fluidly work across multiple forms of input and output—text, image, video, and audio—rather than being limited to just one. These multimodal models are driving a wave of new experiences: AI that can understand a photo and answer questions about it, generate video from text prompts, or engage in spoken dialogue with visual context. Tools like GPT-4o and Gemini reflect this shift, but the real focus here is on the broadening landscape of **capabilities and use cases** that multimodal AI is unlocking.

## Why it matters
Multimodal AI expands what machines can help us do. It enables more intuitive interfaces, like voice-guided smart assistants that see what we see, or creative tools that blend language and imagery in real time. It’s a foundation for assistive technologies, wearables, and context-aware systems that operate across senses and settings. I'm interested in how these models are not only extending human abilities, but also reshaping the boundaries of what counts as natural communication, collaboration, and creativity.
