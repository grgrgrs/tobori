## What this is
The access to the internet has revolved around search engines (e.g. Google, Bing) since its beginning. However, this is rapidly changing as people learn to use chatbots and other AI tools to search for information in a much more robust and focused manner. 
## Why it matters to me
1. I have a long-time interest in information retrieval, where traditional search engines are amazingly poor performers. This is in part technology and likely in part their monetization schemes that aren't aligned with user interests.
2. The pace at which a stalwart component of the internet, search engines, is disappearing is astounding to watch.
3. I may find opportunities to benefit by being slightly ahead of the curve.
4. My system Tobori is an example of AI-powered evolved information search.
